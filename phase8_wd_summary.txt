Findings:
1. wd=5e-4 vs 1e-2 (20x stronger): the sharp m~0 -> m~0.85
   transition survives with nearly identical location
   (midpoint 0.040 vs 0.038; fit eta_c 0.035 vs grid-edge
   0.050). Both show large seed variance right at the jump
   (bistability). So eta_c is NOT fine-tuned to wd=5e-4.
2. beta for wd=1e-2 is not reliably estimable: only 2 eta
   points straddle the jump and m saturates near 0.85, which
   pushes the power-law fit to the eta_c grid edge. The
   model-free midpoint is the trustworthy location estimate.
3. wd=1.0: no trainable ordered phase exists at any eta
   (m=0.20 already at eta=1e-3 with acc 0.58; acc=chance for
   eta>=0.03). The high m is weight-decay pinning weights
   near zero (dead network), physically distinct from the
   above-threshold spectral concentration; no eta_c defined.
